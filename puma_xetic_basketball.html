Abstract

Performance footwear demands faster, more efficient design processes. This thesis develops a computational framework that combines parametric modeling, data-driven iteration, and multi-case FEA to validate auxetic basketball midsoles.

Xetic Basketball serves as the case study for validating the framework across three dynamic movements: running, landing, and cutting.
