Footwear · FEA

Scarpa Vapor V · Finite Element Analysis

Rebuilt the climbing shoe in Rhinoceros, selected layered materials, and simulated multiple load cases in Abaqus to balance stiffness and sensitivity for advanced climbers.

Role
Computational design engineer
Tools
Rhino 6 · Abaqus CAE · Grasshopper
Timeline
4 weeks · solo study
Deliverables
Material report · FEA movies · design recommendations

Overview

Rock climbing shoes act as the interface between athlete and wall; the wrong stiffness profile can limit performance or cause discomfort. The goal of this self-initiated study was to reverse engineer Scarpa's Vapor V, rebuild the geometry, and run iterative FEA scenarios that compare material stacks, midsole cutouts, and pressure distributions under varied stances.

  • Research focus: user pain points, material layering, biomechanical references.
  • Modeling: Rhino + Grasshopper automation for the upper + sole layout.
  • Simulation: Abaqus CAE, nonlinear analysis with hyperelastic materials.

Process

After digitizing the shoe, I built a parametric stack to vary thickness, stiffness, and perforation patterns. Each variant ran through ball-toe load cases to evaluate toe box sensitivity and midsole collapse. Outputs informed design recommendations for future Vapor iterations.

Simulation gallery

Representative snapshots and GIFs from the Abaqus runs showing stress distribution and deformation across different prototypes. Each clip documents the applied load, material combination, and notes on the resulting ride.

Outcomes

The study delivered actionable recommendations for Scarpa's future iterations, demonstrating where to reduce stiffness, how perforation patterns influence energy return, and which materials best balance comfort and control. It also produced a reusable Grasshopper definition to test additional concepts quickly.

  • Identified a midsole perforation pattern that reduces peak stress by 12% while keeping edging support.
  • Built a rendering + GIF library for stakeholders to compare options.
  • Documented a clear handoff for manufacturing partners.
