Experience

During my time at MIT Design Lab, I contributed as a computational designer across multidisciplinary teams. My primary project was Adaptive Dynamics, an industry-sponsored collaboration with PUMA Innovation.

I developed advanced FEM simulations, digital fabrication workflows, and rapid prototypes. A key contribution was rebuilding the virtual design-simulation process to enable faster iteration on auxetic midsoles.

  • Converted 2D midsole FEA to full 3D simulation workflows.
  • Supported rapid iteration for auxetic-based cushioning systems.
  • Collaboration results slated for consumer product + museum exhibition.

Adaptive Dynamics · Xetic
