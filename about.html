Bio

Computational designer, engineer, and founder
based in Milan, Italy

Adaptability has shaped my work from the beginning. I am a computational designer and design engineer with a broad interest in research, emerging technology, and humane innovation.

I was born in Huatusco, Veracruz, Mexico, in a family and region closely connected to making, nature, and resourcefulness. That environment encouraged me to understand how things work and how they could work differently.

Mechatronics Engineering at UNAM gave me a systems-level foundation across mechanics, electronics, programming, and control. Stanford ME310 and my work at Ford Motor Company expanded that foundation into global product development, simulation, manufacturing, and patented engineering solutions.

At Politecnico di Milano, I added design research, product meaning, and interaction design to the engineering toolkit. A research period at MIT Design Lab, followed by collaborations with PUMA Innovation and other international teams, helped me connect computational methods with human performance, emerging interfaces, and strategic innovation.

Today, these different areas converge in Moon Rabbit Lab. I founded the studio to create a space where engineering rigor, design curiosity, simulation, prototyping, and immersive technologies can operate together rather than as isolated specialties.
